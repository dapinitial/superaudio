The questions everyone asks.

If yours isn't here, ask via the waitlist signup — we'll add it.

How is this different from Airfoil?

Airfoil ($35) is a 20-year-old Mac app from Rogue Amoeba. It does multi-protocol Mac→speakers streaming, and it's good at what it does. SuperAudio costs less than half, ships a tvOS companion ($5) so your Apple TV becomes a household hub, includes optional cross-protocol Room Tuning ($5) that doesn't exist in any consumer product, and explicitly supports your stranded old hardware (gen-1 Sonos, B&W A5/A7, AirPort Express, old Apple TVs).

Does my music actually stay in sync between rooms?

Yes — and we measured it. AirPlay 1 receivers sync sample-accurately via our shared timing anchor; Sonos has a hidden internal buffer that we measure with your Mac's microphone and compensate for automatically. Verified 2026-05-18 against B&W A5, B&W A7 (side room), and Sonos Playbar gen 1: a 3-pass chirp calibration through the Mac's built-in mic measured all three speakers' arrival lag at the listening position with SNR 18–33 dB, then aligned them to sub-perceptual sync (under 50 ms). No buttons. The M11 iPhone-mic Room Tuning addon extends this with a walk-around measurement for whole-room frequency correction.

What about my old Sonos? They've been dropping support for older devices.

SuperAudio drives any Sonos that still speaks UPnP — which is every model ever shipped, including the ones Sonos has demoted off their main app. Gen-1 Playbar, Connect, Connect:Amp, Beam, Play:5 — all supported. We don't depend on Sonos's app or cloud; if your Sonos is on the network, we drive it.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Yes. SuperAudio is 100% LAN-based. No cloud, no telemetry, no account required. Your music never leaves your house. Required: a Mac (or our Hub Stick) and a regular WiFi router. No cloud subscription, no Apple ID dance.

What about privacy?

No telemetry. No analytics. No "phoning home." Discovery is LAN-only via Bonjour. Audio streams stay local. We sell the software once and you own it.

I don't have a Mac. Can I still use it?

Yes — the $49 SuperAudio Hub Stick is a tiny preconfigured device that plugs into your router. Setup is one QR code on your phone. Then your iPhone, iPad, or smart TV can AirPlay to the Hub, which fans out to every speaker in your house. No Mac required.
